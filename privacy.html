Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sitewise Studio (“we”, “us”) collects through sitewisestudio.site and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you fill in our contact form, we collect the details you choose to share:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number (optional)
  • The type of website and package you are interested in
  • Whether you are a college student
  • Your message

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you become a client, to deliver your project. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

We do not sell your data

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share it when needed to provide our service (for example, with a domain or hosting provider you ask us to set up) or when required by law.

Form processing

Our contact form is delivered to our email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service. Your submission passes through their servers to reach our inbox. Their own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and for our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing us.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email sitewisestudio.site@gmail.com.
