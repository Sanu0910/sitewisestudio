FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email us or message us on WhatsApp.

Do I get the source code?

Yes, fully. When the project is complete and paid, we hand over the complete source code and all project files. The website is 100% yours to keep, edit or move anywhere.

Is it a one-time payment?

Yes. You pay us once for the website, with no monthly charges from us and no lock-in. Your domain name and hosting renew yearly with the provider you choose (paid directly to them), and we can set those up for you.

How long does it take?

Typically 5–10 working days, depending on the number of pages and how quickly we receive your content (text, photos and logo).

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds of revisions are included. We share the design, you tell us what to change, and we refine it until it feels right within those two rounds.

What are the payment terms?

50% advance to start the project and the remaining 50% before the website goes live. Full source code and ownership are transferred after the final payment.

Is there a student discount?

Yes. College students get a special discount with a valid college ID. Tick “I’m a college student” in the form and we’ll ask you for the ID when we reply.

Can you update my existing site?

Yes. Our Website Redesign service modernises an existing site: new look, mobile-friendly layout, faster loading and cleaner code.
